# Audit K — A2 Money and settlement logic (2026-11-21)

Scope: `server/settlement.ts`, `wallet.ts`, `cod.ts`, `pricing.ts`, `invoice-detail.ts`, `finance-*.ts`, `adminviews.ts` (adjustments, credit line), the money steps of `requests.ts` / `jobs.ts` / `maintenance.ts`, the J4 office-driver payee logic, and a full **reconciliation pass** on the dev DB (`jr_logistics`: 180 days, 2 741 settlements, 15 550 ledger entries, 125 wallets, 2 738 invoices) against the ledger, wallets, settlements, withdrawals, COD remittances, invoices and the analytics / Finance › Drivers numbers.

## Summary

The money core is in good shape: the ledger reconciles to the cent with every wallet, every paid settlement posts four balanced legs to the right payee, escrow matches `requests.reserved_cents` for all 3 124 published requests, and the Finance report, COD report, Finance › Drivers and drivers/money tables tie to each other and to SQL (also for a historical window with Riyadh-day boundaries). **Everything that does not tie is listed below as a finding or a definition difference.**

Real defects found and fixed in this pass (each with a regression test in `web/tests/integration/audit-a2-money.test.ts` + `audit-a2-money-flows.test.ts`; **13 of the 20 tests fail on the pre-fix code** — the other 7 pin safety that was already there: races on withdrawals / decisions / cash receipts / publishes and the property-style books check):

* **3 high**: escrow stuck for ever when a part-finished request is cancelled; a fractional VAT rate (accepted by the API) would make every payout fail; driverless rentals were charged without any invoice / VAT record (51 charges, SAR 52 848 in the dev data).
* **4 medium**: a payout error rolled back the driver's finished delivery; held settlements starved the payout window; settlement VAT drifted from the invoice VAT; money sums cast to `int` overflow beyond SAR 21.4 M.
* **5 low**: replayed escrow release drifted the request counter; concurrent price edits created two approved versions; reused adjustment idempotency key silently ignored; a settlement "hold" on a complaint with no order; full IBAN shown to `finance.view` staff.
* **Open, needs migration (A4/lead): 1 high (latent)** — `wallet_accounts` balances are `integer`; the platform wallet only ever grows and stops every payout at SAR 21.47 M.

**Definition differences (not defects, now written down):** *Platform revenue* on Finance overview = platform wallet balance (VAT-inclusive margin + rentals − adjustments), Finance report `fin_revenue` = VAT-inclusive margin of settlements created in the period incl. unpaid, excl. rentals, `fin_net_position` = margin − VAT. *Wallet liability*: overview = Σ available (signed) + held (SAR 93 271, escrow shown separately), report = positive balances + held + escrow (SAR 124 933); credit in use is a receivable in both. `fin_credit_*` ignore the period by definition ("as of now"). `adminStats` GMV = paid settlements, all time.

## Needs owner decision (recommended defaults)

1. **Balance of a driver who joins an office** (finding 14). Default: at the moment of linking, show staff the residual balance in Finance › Drivers and let the driver withdraw *that* balance once (no new earnings), instead of freezing it. Alternative: refuse the link while the wallet is non-zero. Not implemented (touches J4).
2. **Zero-value settlements** (18). Default: no tax invoice and no ledger rows when `customer = 0` (the settlement still closes the job). Not implemented: the demo verifier and `invoice` tests assume one invoice per paid settlement.
3. **Unremitted cash vs withdrawals.** A driver holding SAR 8 000 of cash can withdraw his whole wallet; nothing links the two. Default: show "cash held by this driver" on the withdrawal approval row (no automatic block). C8 says "no COD" while E17 later added it — keep E17.
4. **VAT in "platform revenue"** (the wallet and the report include the VAT that is owed to the tax authority). Default: show net-of-VAT as the headline once the tax owner confirms (#5). ZATCA / seller legal name still open (H6).
5. **B3 / B8** (call-out fee, demurrage, zone surcharge) are "approved" but absent and contradict #8 — confirm they are out of scope.
6. **A company cannot withdraw unused wallet balance** (only staff adjustments). Default: keep, add a staff "refund to bank" flow when a payment provider exists.

## Cross-area

* **A4** (migration): finding 13 (`bigint` wallet balances); `ledger_entries` should be append-only (trigger refusing UPDATE/DELETE); `invoices` has no `UNIQUE (settlement_id) WHERE settlement_id IS NOT NULL` — one invoice per settlement rests on the settlement row lock; findings 15 and 16 (analytics).
* **A1**: `GET /admin/finance/withdrawals` item mask (done, see 12); `adjustWallet` / `receiveCash` are not rate limited (permission gated only).
* **A5/A6**: double-submit protection on withdraw, cash receive, top-up buttons (17).
* **A3**: `cancelRequest` locks the request then offers, while driver actions lock offer then request (`lockOffer` → `lockRequest`): a deadlock victim returns 500 (retry works); existing race tests pass.

## Checked and fine

* Ledger posting (`wallet.post`): idempotent on the unique key, balance update + CHECK (`available + creditLimit ≥ 0`, reserved/held ≥ 0) in the same transaction; two withdrawals racing for one balance, pay vs reject of one withdrawal, two cash receipts, the same adjustment ×3, two publishes against one balance, two payout ticks, top-up replays: all end with exactly one winner and balanced books (tests).
* Lock order is the same everywhere (business → payee → platform wallet; request → wallet), the platform account is created under an advisory lock, `nextSeq` is a transactional counter (gapless, no number burned by a rolled-back payout).
* VAT: `round(total × p / (100 + p))`, property-tested 5 000 random totals × 6 rates (|error| ≤ ½ halala, subtotal + VAT = total), identical to Postgres `round()` on all 2 738 invoices; never floats in storage (`integer` halalas, `fmtMoney` only for display).
* Pricing: `CHECK price > 0 and cost ≤ price`, versions immutable, snapshot at publish (`quote`), settlement uses the snapshot (0 mismatches), edit = new version.
* Escrow: reserve at publish (credit line aware), release at payout / cancel / expire / all-or-nothing call-off / unfilled slots / completion, parcel-level cancel; `syncOfferUnits` keeps Σ offer shares ≤ reserve.
* Office payee = snapshot `offers.office_id`; joining/leaving is refused with an active job and expires offered offers, so the payee cannot be wrong; office drivers' earnings are hidden in views, notifications and timelines.
* COD never touches the wallet; receipts are serialised by the offer row lock; a remittance can never exceed what is outstanding.
* Invoice detail / company sheet: lines derived and reconciled to the total, company payload carries no cost/margin/payee, owner-scoped.
* Finance › Drivers: phone masked, search by phone refused without `drivers.sensitive`, period windows on Riyadh days, CSV/xlsx audited.
* Settings validation: top-up/withdrawal/credit/adjustment amounts are bounded `int` ≤ 5·10⁸–10⁹ (below int4), Postgres range errors map to 422.
