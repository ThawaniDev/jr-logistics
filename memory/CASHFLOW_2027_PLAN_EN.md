# JR Logistics — Funding plan: nothing from your pocket after the first 3 months

*Companion of `CASHFLOW_2027_EN.md`, same assumptions: 10 drivers at 40 shipments a day, 26 working days a month, 30% monthly growth, 7 / 6 / 1 SAR, capital 50,000 SAR, warehouses billed at month-end and **paying on the 5th of the next month**. All amounts in SAR, excluding VAT.*

## 1. The plan in short

**Why you were short:** the second half of the drivers was paid on the last day of the month, 5 days **before** that month's warehouse money arrived on the 5th. So you paid a whole month of driver pay in advance, and that month grows 30% every month (481,752 by December).

**The fix is only a payout date:** pay the drivers **after** the warehouses pay you.

1. **Pay every 15 days on the 6th and the 21st.** The 6th pays the second half (16–end) of the month that just closed; the 21st pays the first half (1–15) of the current month. The warehouse money arrives on the 5th, before both payouts. Drivers wait 6 days after their period closes; the rhythm stays every 15 days.
2. **From your pocket: 0 for driver pay.** Your 50,000 SAR capital is enough for the whole year (lowest cash **18,800** on 21 Jan 2027; **77,430** on 31 Dec 2027). Use your first 3 months of funding only for what this model does not contain: **your fixed costs** (section 5).
3. **If you want to launch with the faster payout (15th and last day) for the first 3 months**, put in **31,536** (minimum) or **50,000** (with a reserve) between January and March and switch to the 6th / 21st on 1 April. After that nothing more from your pocket, and from May you can take your money back (section 4).
4. **Rule:** release the 6th payout only when the warehouse money is in the bank (section 6).
5. **Limits:** with the amounts above it works up to about **35% monthly growth** (your plan: 30%). At 40% the amount you would have to put in by March jumps to 186,548 (section 5).

**Late warehouses.** The warehouses must pay by the 5th. The 6th payout (43% of the month's billing) still works even if 63% of last month's billing is unpaid on the 6th. The 21st payout is tighter: by the 21st at least **93% of the previous month's billing** must be in the bank (in December). A warehouse that pays after the 21st would stop the first-half payout, so enforce the credit limit (section 7).

## 6. Rules that keep you out of your pocket

1. **Pay after you are paid.** Release the 6th payout only when the warehouse money for the closed month is in the bank (if it arrives on the 8th, pay on the 8th). Never advance driver pay from your own money.
2. **Check the cash before every payout:** cash after the payout must stay at or above a reserve (suggested 15,000).
3. **Invoice on the last day, due on the 5th.** A warehouse that is late gets no new orders until it pays (the platform's credit limit does this).
4. **Cap growth** at about 35% a month: add drivers only as the warehouse money arrives.
5. **VAT** collected with the invoices is not your cash: keep it in a separate account for ZATCA.
6. Review the cash against the table in section 2 on the 21st of every month.

## 7. What this needs in the JR platform

* **Driver payouts** are a routine: Finance exports the withdrawal requests (Finance › Withdrawals, bank file) and marks the batch paid on the **6th and the 21st**. Nothing in the platform pays on its own, so the change is only the dates of that routine. Tell drivers the payout days when they register.
* **Warehouse credit limit:** a company can be given a credit limit (its balance may go below zero up to that amount). Each warehouse needs a limit of **about one month plus 5 days of its billing**.
* **Not built yet:** the monthly statement per warehouse, due date (the 5th) and overdue handling (gap L6 in `memory/audit/gaps.md`). Until then, send the statement by hand on the last day and follow up on the 5th.
